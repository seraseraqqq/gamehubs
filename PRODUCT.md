# GameHubs

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Independent browser games on separate single-game sites. The owner prioritizes organic search acquisition and eventual advertising revenue, without separate consumer brands.

## Users
Visitors seeking an immediately playable browser game. For the current Coreball redesign, the owner confirmed retaining the timing-puzzle rules and strengthening cat interaction and game feel.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Coreball has 30 original rotating-pin levels, Challenge and slower Practice, pointer/touch/keyboard controls and browser-local progress. Preserve existing saves and keep the game playable without accounts or installation. Each game is an isolated static deployment. Formal domains and ad integrations for Coreball are not configured in the repository.

## Brand Commitments
Use Coreball as the primary SEO term, no Pinlark brand. The owner requests a three-dimensional fluffy ball and cat actions. During live iteration the owner rejected the seated side-cat and dark surface ripples; after trying pixel paws, the owner explicitly rejected their duck-foot ambiguity. The latest direction is smooth 2D flat paws seen vertically from above, no oblique perspective, coordinated toy-pin art and stronger localized impact feedback. Cat reactions accompany shooting and results; they do not obstruct the core puzzle. Other games remain outside this redesign.

## Evidence on Hand
Original engine, 30 level configurations, tests and existing source in games/coreball. SEO page map and metadata are documented in research/2026-10-23-keyword-seo. No ranking, traffic or revenue claim is established.

## Product Principles
- Let visitors start playing from the homepage.
- Keep the advertised search intent consistent with the actual game.
- Make feedback enjoyable without changing the core collision rules.
- Preserve keyboard use, reduced-motion preferences and safe local storage.
