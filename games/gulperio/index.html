A neon arena. Your own pace.

This independent, single-player arena runs against computer-controlled snakes. Collect energy orbs, grow carefully and try to beat your personal best.

  • Steer with the mouse or drag on the canvas. A second finger activates touch boost.
  • Hold the left mouse button or Space with the arena focused to boost. Release to stop spending length.

  • Start another run from the result screen or the New run button. Arena rankings describe this local match.

Questions before one more round?

Am I playing against other people?

No. This is a single-player snake arena with computer-controlled opponents. The arena rankings are local to this run, not a global or live multiplayer leaderboard.

How do I steer and boost?

Move your mouse over the arena to steer, or drag a finger on the canvas. Hold the left mouse button or Space while the arena has focus to boost. On touchscreens, use a second finger to boost. Boosting spends some length.

Can I pass through my own tail?

Yes. You can cross your own body. Hitting another snake with your head or reaching the arena boundary ends your run.

Is this the official Gulper.io game?

No. This is an independent browser game inspired by snake-arena mechanics. It has its own computer-controlled arena and does not connect to the original Gulper.io service.

What does the site save?

Your personal best and sound setting stay in this browser when storage is available. Clearing site data removes them. The current run and arena rankings are not saved.

Will it work on every school or work network?

Access depends on your network rules. A browser-based game and its domain cannot guarantee access or bypass a network filter.

Rivals with a little personality

How do the computer rivals play?

Each rival has its own play style: collecting, hunting, drifting or taking rookie risks. They react to nearby food and snakes, sometimes sprint, change their minds and make mistakes. They are computer-controlled, not online players.
