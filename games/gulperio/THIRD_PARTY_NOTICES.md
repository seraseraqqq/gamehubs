# Asset and service notes

Dela Gothic One and Figtree Latin font subsets are hosted locally under the
SIL Open Font License. Their full notices are in assets/fonts/*-OFL.txt.

Game icons, social cards and the category/arena vector illustrations added in
the 2026-10-23 audit are original HTML/CSS/SVG artwork. Category illustrations
are symbolic hints, not official retailer photography. Product names in the
price-puzzle catalog identify reference game items; prices have no verified
collection date and are not live store quotes.

The existing optional Vercel Web Analytics / Speed Insights integrations are
retained for public hosting, with a visible privacy note. Local previews do
not request Vercel-only analytics endpoints. No advertising was added.
