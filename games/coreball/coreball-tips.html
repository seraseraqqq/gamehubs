Coreball Tips & Level Guide: All 30 Original Levels

Winning a Coreball-style level means attaching every incoming pin without touching a pin already on the core. This edition has 30 original patterns. There is no single click rhythm that works for all of them: later groups vary the rotation speed during each turn.

Time the landing, not the click

Each pin travels for 0.24 seconds before it reaches the core. The board keeps rotating during that flight. Watch where the next open gap is moving and launch before it reaches the bottom entry point. Wait for the current pin to land before firing again.

After a collision, restart and watch one rotation before your first shot. Try a wider opening rather than repeating the same rapid clicks. As successful shots fill the board, pause between launches and reassess the available space.

Learn a pattern at 70% speed

Practice slows the core’s rotation to 70% of its Challenge speed; pin flight time stays the same. Learn the direction and changes in pace there, then adjust your timing in Challenge. Practice clears have separate records and do not unlock Challenge completions.

Touch, mouse and keyboard controls

  • Tap the field or click Launch a pin to shoot.
  • With the game focused, Space or Enter launches; P pauses; R restarts.
  • Only one pin can be in flight. Extra taps during flight do not queue shots.
  • Leaving the browser tab pauses play. Progress is local to this browser when storage is available.

Why do repeated clicks fail?

The core can turn a different distance between two shots, especially in later levels. A previously safe rhythm may now put a pin beside an existing one. Slow down, observe a full turn and choose each opening individually.
