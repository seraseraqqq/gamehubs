How to play this
Coreball-style game.

Coreball-style games test your timing: attach every pin to a rotating core without hitting an existing pin. This version uses its own 30 level patterns. Tap the playing field, use the launch button, or press Space or Enter while the game is focused. A pin in flight must land before the next launch.

Challenge runs at full speed. Practice slows the same original patterns to 70%; it has separate progress. Pause whenever you like, and restart with a fresh board. Leaving the browser tab pauses the game.

Coreball tips:
watch the landing.

The pin takes a moment to reach the spinning core. Aim for the gap that will be at the bottom when it lands, rather than the gap you see when you click. Fire one pin at a time and reassess as the board fills.

Try Practice at 70% speed to learn a pattern, then return to Challenge for a separate completion record. Our Coreball tips and 30-level reference explains changing speeds, crowded boards and the controls. All levels are available from the start.

Coreball game: frequently asked questions

Is this the original Coreball game?

No. This is an independent timing game inspired by rotating-pin puzzle mechanics. Its code, artwork and 30 level configurations were created for this project. It does not use Coreball’s level data or services.

What is saved?

Your last selected level, sound preference, attempts, completed levels and best successful times stay in this browser when storage is available. An in-progress shot does not resume after a refresh.

Can I play with touch or a keyboard?

Yes. Tap the field or launch button. With the game focused, Space or Enter launches, P pauses, and R restarts. Page navigation and text controls keep their normal keyboard behavior.

Does Practice unlock Challenge results?

No. Practice is slower and has separate local records. Only successful Challenge rounds count toward the 30 Challenge completions.

How many levels are there?

This edition has 30 original, replayable levels. The timing patterns change between levels; there is no daily play limit.
