How do I jump and climb in Cluster Rush?

Press Space or J to jump. Hold the same key to cling and climb when you meet a truck’s side. Use A/D or the arrow keys to move left and right.

Is Cluster Rush free, and does it need a download?

You can play the embedded browser game for free, without an installer or account. Your browser still downloads the Unity engine and game assets before play.

Is this Cluster Rush unblocked?

Network access depends on your school, workplace, or connection. This page cannot override their restrictions. See loading help for normal browser and connection checks.
