Privacy & game provider

Last updated: October 24, 2026.

Before you press play

This website serves its page files and font locally. The page does not include advertising scripts, analytics trackers or a sign-in form. Hosting services may process ordinary request logs, such as an IP address, browser information and request time.

After you press play

The game connects to GamePix and the services used by its embedded player. Those services can process connection information, use browser storage and show advertisements or privacy choices. Review the choices inside the player before continuing. Website focus mode does not hide or override those choices.

Read GamePix’s privacy information and the provider’s contact page for provider-related requests. The game’s loading or preference interface may also link to its current privacy and cookie notices.

Game progress and online play

This page does not create a player account, collect a score or synchronize progress. Any saves or multiplayer data handled by the embedded game are controlled by the provider. Clearing browser data may remove locally stored game progress.

External links

Opening GamePix directly or following another external link takes you to a separately operated website. Its privacy practices apply there.
