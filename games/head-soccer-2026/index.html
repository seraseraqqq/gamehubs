Head Soccer 2026 turns football into a fast one-on-one duel. Pick a national team, keep an eye on the ball and use jumps, headers and two shot buttons to get it past your opponent.

Start in single-player to learn the timing. For a same-keyboard rematch, choose 2 Players in the game menu and share the screen with a friend. The controls panel above switches between the two keyboard layouts without restarting your match.

Before the whistle.

Can I play Head Soccer 2026 for free?

Yes. You can open the browser game here without paying or installing an app. The game is delivered by GamePix and may display its own advertising or privacy choices.

How do I play with two players?

Choose 2 Players inside the game on a computer. Player 1 uses A/D to move, W to jump and N/M to shoot. Player 2 uses the arrow keys and 1/2 to shoot. Both players share one keyboard.

Can I play on my phone?

The provider lists mobile support. Use the on-screen movement, jump and shot buttons. Landscape orientation gives the pitch more room; a computer is recommended for local two-player matches.

Why is the game taking time to load?

The game downloads from GamePix after you press play. A slow connection, content blocker or privacy prompt can delay it. Use Reload game or the direct link beneath the player. No game download is bundled with this website.

Does online multiplayer work?

Some versions show an Online Multiplayer option. Connection and matchmaking depend on the provider's servers. We have verified local single-player play; remote matchmaking is not guaranteed.
