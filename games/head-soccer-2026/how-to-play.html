How to play
Head Soccer 2026

Keep the ball out of your own goal and put it into your opponent’s. Move, jump and time your shots to finish ahead when the match timer ends. Start with a solo match, then try sharing a keyboard with a friend.

Start a local two-player match

  1. Choose Play in the embedded game, then choose 2 Players.
  2. Choose your players or national teams.
  3. Share one computer keyboard. Player 1 takes the letter keys; Player 2 takes the arrows and number keys.
  4. Score more goals than your friend before the match ends. Start a rematch from the game menu.

The controls selector on this website only changes the instructions. Choose the actual match mode inside the game itself.

Three things to practise

Leave room behind the ball

Chasing every rebound can leave your goal exposed. When the ball rises, move back into position and watch where it will land.

Jump with a purpose

Use a jump to meet a high ball or block its path. Repeated jumps without watching the ball make it easier to lose your position.

Try both shot buttons

Learn how Z and X affect the ball from different positions in single-player mode. Timing and distance matter as much as the button you press.

Playing on a phone or tablet

The provider lists the game as mobile compatible. Turn your device to landscape and use the game’s own touch arrows and shot buttons. Fullscreen availability depends on the browser; Focus mode expands the player on this page when browser fullscreen is unavailable.

If the game is blank or will not start

  • Wait for GamePix to finish loading, then use its start button and review any privacy options it shows.
  • Check your connection. Browser content restrictions may prevent the provider from loading.
  • Use Reload game below the player, or open the direct GamePix link. Reloading ends the current match.
  • If keyboard input scrolls the page, click inside the player again. Use the game menu for pause and sound controls.

About online multiplayer

Some releases include an Online Multiplayer menu item. Its availability depends on the provider’s network services and an opponent being available. This website does not run its own matchmaking server. Remote play has not been verified in our checks.
