A little space. One more move.

Fit the three tray shapes onto the 8×8 board. A complete row or column clears, making room for the next piece. Clear lines on consecutive placements to build your combo.

  • Keep an open area for larger pieces and check all three shapes before placing one.
  • Drag to place, or select a piece and tap its top-left starting cell.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a tray piece and press Enter. Arrow keys move the board cursor; Enter places the piece; Escape clears selection.
  • There is no round timer. Take a moment to plan your next placement.

Questions before one more round?

How do I place a block?

Drag a shape onto the board, or select a tray piece and tap an empty starting cell. With a keyboard, Tab to a piece and press Enter, then use the arrow keys and Enter on the board.

Can the pieces rotate?

No. Each piece keeps the orientation shown in the tray. Try a different space or place another shape first.

When does a game end?

The game ends when none of the remaining tray shapes fits anywhere on the board. Completing a row or column clears those cells.

What is saved?

Only your best score and sound preference are saved on this device. The current board does not resume after a refresh. Storage restrictions or clearing site data can remove the record.

Does unblocked guarantee access on every network?

No. This is a browser-based game with no account or download requirement. Access still depends on the rules and filters of your network.
