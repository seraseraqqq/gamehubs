Acceleration City / About & privacy

A focused page for a creative driving game.

This is an independent player page and guide. Acceleration City was developed by Carnegie Mellon Robotics Academy; this page is not affiliated with or endorsed by Carnegie Mellon University or CS2N.

The game and its creators

The game combines open-world driving, races and a programmable CoBot. The academy lists it in the official CoBox games directory and describes it in its Hour of Code resources. All original game names, art and code remain the property of their respective owners.

The original game displays “For educational use only.” This local prototype is for evaluation. Commercial embedding permission has not been established; no permission or partnership is claimed.

What loads when you press Play

Pressing Play opens the original CS2N activity in an embedded frame. The game is loaded from CS2N, rather than copied to this website. Reload reopens that frame. The Focus view and Fullscreen buttons change the surrounding presentation.

Privacy and local data

This page adds no advertising, analytics, account system or cookies. It does not access your game score, account, save files or the contents of the embedded page.

The game is a separate third-party service. When you press Play, your browser connects to CS2N and may load its own code, storage and third-party services. Their behavior is controlled by the original provider and your browser settings. View the CS2N website for its current policies and official support channels.

Scope of this guide

Instructions describe the browser activity and official educational materials available during research on October 24, 2026. The embedded game can change independently. The guide makes no claim of universal mobile support, network bypass, multiplayer availability or guaranteed saving.
