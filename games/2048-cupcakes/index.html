Can I keep playing after 2048?

Absolutely. Reach the rainbow cupcake to win, then choose Keep baking. There's a golden encore at 4096, and the numbers can keep growing after that.

Will my batch be here when I come back?

Your current board, score, best score and preferences stay in this browser's local storage. They don't sync across devices. Clearing site data or using some private modes can remove them.

How do Undo and New batch work?

Undo restores your previous board and score, including the cupcake added after that move. You can step back through the last 10 valid moves. New batch starts a fresh board; your best score stays.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

This version is free to play and needs no account. It has no ads, analytics or third-party game embeds.
