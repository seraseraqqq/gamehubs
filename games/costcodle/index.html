How to Play Costcodle Unlimited

  • Study: Look at the product name, packaging specs, and size details.
  • Guess: Enter your estimate of the product's actual warehouse dollar price.
  • Feedback: Arrows indicate if the price is higher (↑) or lower (↓).
  • Colors: Red is off by >25%, Yellow is within 25%, and Green is within 5% (Win!).
  • Unlimited: Switch to Unlimited Mode anytime to practice with dozens of real warehouse items.
What is Costcodle Unlimited?

Costcodle Unlimited is a free price-guessing trivia game. While the classic daily mode gives you one puzzle per day, Unlimited Mode allows endless continuous play with full streak and score tracking.

How do the temperature colors and arrows work?

After each guess, an arrow tells you whether the true price is higher (↑) or lower (↓). Red means cold (over 25% away), yellow means warm (within 25%), and green means you are within 5%—scoring a win!

Are the product prices based on real Costco stores?

Yes. Prices are curated from real US Costco warehouse price tags across food court classics, Kirkland Signature groceries, electronics, and seasonal items.

Can I share my score with friends?

Yes! When you complete a round, click the Share button to copy a spoiler-free emoji grid (just like Wordle) to paste into group chats or social media.
