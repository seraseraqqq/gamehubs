Questions before one more round?

How do I win?

Enter a positive dollar amount. A guess within 5% of the stored puzzle price wins. Otherwise an arrow tells you whether the target is higher or lower. You have six guesses.

When does the daily challenge reset?

The daily item is selected using the UTC calendar date. Reload after midnight UTC for the next challenge. Unlimited mode lets you keep playing from the same reference catalog.

Are these current store prices?

No. These are fixed reference values in the game catalog, without a verified collection date. They are not live prices, offers or shopping advice. Actual prices vary by location and date.

Why are the pictures illustrations?

The category illustrations are made for this game and hosted locally. They are visual hints, not official product photographs. Use the complete product name and package size when making your guess.

What is stored on my device?

Your statistics, completed daily results and sound preference are stored in this browser when storage is available. They do not sync to another device and can be removed by clearing site data.

Is this an official Costco game?

No. This is an independent fan-made price puzzle. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Costco Wholesale Corporation.
