## Product and SEO decisions

Build original downhill sledding with real WebGL 3D, continuous steering, jumps, trees/rocks/logs, collectible gifts, increasing speed, local best and unlockable sled colors. Clearly state independent version before play; do not imply affiliation or universal network access. No account, ad SDK or external runtime requests. Three.js is a locally pinned MIT renderer, not a third-party game.

Homepage owns the main play query. One useful supporting guide owns controls and practical tips; no thin pages for 66/76/911 variants. Title/description/H1 remain truthful about the independent build. JSON-LD uses VideoGame without fake reviews or invented ratings. Owned-origin build enables canonical, absolute OG URL, sitemap and indexable robots; previews remain noindex.

## Design direction

Mode: Experience. Seven grounded systems considered: alpine piste signage; winter sports broadcast; snow-park trail map; ski school handbook; lift-ticket typography; Nordic outdoor catalog; mountain refuge window. The direction seed assigned candidate 7: mountain refuge window. Actual tool output seed key `402b3f77`, approved pool `c3b204a1eed6`, `--scope direction --mode experience`, assigned index 7. A single expansive view onto the original snowy forest leads, framed by a quiet warm-white shell, evergreen controls, glacier sky and red gift accents. Navigation and controls remain ordinary accessible web elements. The first viewport proves playability.

Five direction promises: THESIS — an expansive view onto a playable original downhill; FORM — a single mountain-window game field framed by a quiet shell; FIRST VIEWPORT — independence disclosure, start, score and actual 3D scene are visible together; OWN-WORLD — evergreen, warm-white, glacier sky and red sled/gifts; STORY — fresh slope, one run, gifts and another attempt. No catalog world was selected, so there is no claimed catalog quality-bar image or approved comp.

Challenger judgments: green capsules declined (less winter identification/clarity; retain palette commitment); orienteering map competitive on identification but weaker for immediate play (retain control legend clarity); moon-shadow bazaar declined (unrelated to player task; retain atmospheric depth). Code-led build as configured in this repository. No user-facing concept approval gate: user explicitly delegated research → design → development.
